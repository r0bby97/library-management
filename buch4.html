Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

Author Joanne K. Rowling
Release year 2000
Average rating 4,7
Add to Wishlist


The school finds itself playing host to two visiting magical schools. Only three of the bravest witches or wizards over the age of seventeen may compete; their names to be drawn out of the enticing, golden Triwizard Cup. But this is no Quidditch match, the reward is glory but the perils are lethal and somebody has other plans in store. For when the time comes, the unthinkable happens; an extra name is recalled from the cup, Harry Potter’s name.
